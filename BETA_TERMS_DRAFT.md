## 2. The service
Vexmera is an AI-powered marketing platform intended to help businesses connect marketing and analytics data, understand performance, identify opportunities and receive recommended next actions.

The private beta is a pre-release service. Features may be incomplete, changed, removed or temporarily unavailable. Provider approvals, permissions and API availability may limit functionality.

## 3. Read-only advertising integrations during beta
During the private beta, supported advertising integrations are intended to be read-only unless Vexmera explicitly states otherwise in writing for a separately reviewed feature.

The beta must not autonomously:
- Publish ads
- Create live campaigns
- Change budgets
- Change bids
- Pause or activate campaigns
- Modify targeting
- Make other external advertising-account changes

Vexmera may generate recommendations, draft campaign proposals and prepare approval items while external execution remains disabled. Any later execution feature must require separate safeguards, appropriate provider permissions, account-level testing and clear customer controls.

## 4. Accounts and access
Users are responsible for:

Users must not attempt to access another customer's workspace or circumvent security controls.

## 14. Customer controls, disconnect and deletion
The private-beta product separates different controls that have different consequences:

### Disconnect a supported Google or Meta account
Disconnecting removes Vexmera's locally stored connector credential and saved connector/account configuration, stops future synchronization through that credential and attempts provider-side revocation where supported. Previously synchronized reporting history is not automatically deleted by disconnecting.

### Delete synchronized marketing history
An authorized workspace owner or administrator may separately delete supported synchronized campaign/KPI history after an explicit destructive confirmation. Manually entered KPI rows are intentionally outside that scoped deletion operation.

### Delete the Vexmera account
The product includes a guarded self-service account-deletion flow requiring current-password re-authentication and exact final confirmation. Deletion is blocked if an owned workspace still has other members or has an active attached subscription. When deletion is allowed, eligible solo-owned local workspace/account data is deleted and memberships in other owners' workspaces are removed without deleting those shared business workspaces.

Provider-side token revocation is best-effort. Third-party billing, accounting, security or compliance records may remain where Vexmera or its processors are required or permitted to retain them.

The final legally reviewed Privacy Policy and any DPA must control the detailed data-handling description.

## 15. Suspension and termination

Termination of the service relationship does not automatically require immediate deletion of every record held by third-party processors where retention is necessary for accounting, disputes, fraud prevention, security or legal obligations.

No system can guarantee absolute security. Users must use reasonable account-security practices and report suspected compromise promptly.
