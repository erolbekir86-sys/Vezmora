## 2. The service
Vexmera is an AI-powered marketing platform intended to help businesses connect marketing and analytics data, understand performance, identify opportunities and receive recommended next actions.

The private beta is a pre-release service. Features may be incomplete, changed, removed or temporarily unavailable.

## 3. Read-only advertising integrations during beta
During the private beta, supported advertising integrations are intended to be read-only unless Vexmera explicitly states otherwise in writing for a particular beta feature.

The beta must not autonomously:
- Publish ads
- Create live campaigns
- Change budgets
- Change bids
- Pause or activate campaigns
- Modify targeting
- Make other external advertising-account changes

Any future execution feature should require separate safeguards, appropriate provider permissions and clear customer controls.
